# Mindjack: Unique Weapons Enhanced

## About

Mindjack: Unique Weapons Enhanced is a mod that expands a little on both the Unique Weapon system as well as the biocoding and weapon bonding system, with it's own unique twins. Register yourself to the rudimentary AI by connecting your 
Mindjack to the Weapon's Port, ehancing your combat compabilities as well as your overall state of mind. Becareful, however, as unregistered users that try to use the now registered weapon might find it harder than if the AI
was still dormant!

## Lore

Before the time of the Archotech, before the time of the Mech Hive, our understanding of AI and it's capabilities was infantile. Calling it AI would be considered a joke nowadays, as it was far from intelligent of self aware. Uses for AI was everywhere, so it was only a matter of time until it made it's way into military
applications. Crude by today's standards, a combat AI was installed on weapons to try and enhance their effectiveness, but another piece of the puzzle was missing. Enter: the Mindjack. An arguably poorly designed but effective
implant, installed directy into the user's brain, aloow the AI to register the user's unique brain markup, enhancing both the AI and the user, at a cost. While not dangerous on it's own, removal of the Mindjack was - and still is - 
considered an impossibility, as any attempt at surgically removing the device would result in irreperable brain damage, and in most cases, instant death.

Eventually, the technology was lost to time, overtaken by the glitterworld technology we know of today. However, somewhere and somehow, the AI was re-discovered, and distribution of AI powered weapons circulated into the market. Installing
the Mindjack is a permanent mark on yourself, following you until the end of your life. Perhaps one day, a new and better implant will be developed, and a surgical technique will allow for the Mindjack to be removed as any other impant.
For now, we do what we can to make do. In the Rim, every single bullet counts

## Features

* Craft a Mindjack on your Pawns by researching Microelectronics (or it's own research I haven't decided yet) or find them sold by some merchants. As long as you have one, you can install it on any Pawn you want
* The Mindjack is a permanent addition to your Pawn, but on it's own it's harmless.
* Register your Mind Link with any Industrial era or higher Unique Weaponry, forever tying the Weapon to your pawn.
* Registering a weapon will set it's quality to Good if it's of a higher quality. However, as long as the Pawn has their registered weapon equipped, the weapon's quality will be Legendary. Not only that, but your pawn will enjoy a flat 10% Consciousness buff.
* While a weapon can only register a single unique user, the Mindjack can be formatted, allowing the user to be able to register a new weapon upon complition, at the cost of Consciousness being set maxxed at 90% while the formatting process happens over several days.
* (TBD) Become a victim of a Mindjack raid, led by a raider holding a registered weapon. If you kill them, you still get a Good quality Unique Weapon. However, you might want to consider keeping them alive, and get the most out of the weapon once you get your hands on it

## Known issues
* Pawns incapable of violence can register the weapon, but cannot equip it. I will try to change this for release if possible
* Ports can spawn on Neolithic and Medieval weapons. I don't think I need to explain why this is an issue
